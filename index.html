What's the story behind my travel blog?
It started as a way to keep track of my own trips and slowly turned into something bigger. What began as a handful of photos and notes for friends has grown into a place where I share the destinations, people, and moments that have shaped how I see the world. Every story here comes from a place I've actually visited myself.
How do I plan, document, and share my travels?
Every trip starts with a loose itinerary that leaves plenty of room for spontaneity. Along the way I keep notes and take photos, then turn them into the articles you find here once I'm back home.
How can brands, companies, or fellow creators collaborate with me or get in touch?
I'm always open to collaborations that fit the spirit of this blog, whether that's a joint trip, a sponsored feature, or something else entirely. Just use the contact form below and tell me a bit about your idea, and I'll get back to you.
How can readers support this blog and the work behind it?
Reading, sharing, and commenting on the articles already helps more than you'd think. If you'd like to support the blog more directly, following along on social media and passing posts on to fellow travelers means a lot.
